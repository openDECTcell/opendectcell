| works at 8, rejected at 6 | the padding differs (left vs right alignment, or 0x0 vs 0xF) |

### Test 10 result: **wideband works on 07.56** -- pre-committed row 1 `[vRINGA540-20261025-100438-1]`

Valid run: `{CC-ALERTING}` chose G.722 long slot (`7c 04 90 03 00 81`), ring
on a fresh link ("page response received"), one bind `0x0230 .. 41 02 00 00
00`, no re-assign. Heard: clear echo 0.5 s late, "sounds wideband"; the A540
shows no HD symbol (it probably has none).

Capture (all 8 TDM slots, 50.5 s of call), word 0:
- repeats every two frames **50.2 %** of the time: the coded lane, 8000
  octets/s = G.722 at 64 kbit/s;
- read as linear PCM: flat noise, ~16 000 RMS every second -- not linear;
- decoded as G.722 (`tools/dect_g722_extract.py`, ffmpeg): speech bursts up
  to 6600 RMS, silence floor 3-15;
- **frames at 3.9, 8.5 and 11.1 s carry 90-99 % of their energy in
  4-7 kHz** (sibilants), which a narrowband path cuts at 3.4 kHz. The
  7-8 kHz energy in the first second is a decoder start-up artefact.

So stock's bind (flags 0x41, codec byte 0) gives the coded G.722 path on
07.56 with a Gigaset, as on 08.25 with the C4 (v31). What is left for
wideband calls is our own downlink: G.722 from the host paced to the chip's
two-frame words (v32 "worked, with glitches", `playsync`), and choosing
G.722 instead of G.726 in our `{CC-CONNECT}` for handset-placed calls.
